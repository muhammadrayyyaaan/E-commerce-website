Our Story

In 2020, three friends couldn't find football boots that survived a full season. So they made their own. Then a jersey. Then a hoodie. Then a movement.

Today, APEX is worn in 42 countries by athletes who care less about logos and more about the work. We're not chasing trends — we're chasing the next training session.

What We Stand For

Three Rules

01

Make It Real

Every product is tested by athletes, not focus groups. If it doesn't survive a real session, it doesn't ship. No exceptions.

02

Own The Work

We design, prototype and quality-check in-house. We know every stitch, every panel, every athlete who tested it.

03

Build The Squad

Our customers aren't a market. They're the squad. We listen, we learn, we build the next drop together.

The Workshop

From a garage
to 42 countries.

It started in a converted garage in East London with a sewing machine bought off Facebook Marketplace. Six prototypes, three rejected suppliers, one all-nighter that turned into a hundred.

We never raised outside money. We never compromised on materials. Every cent we earned went back into making the next product better than the last. That's still how we operate.
